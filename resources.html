Where zoning, entitlement and development capital actually happen.

Last checked: November 2, 2026

Zoning decides what can be built, entitlement turns that decision into a permission, and development finance prices the result. The links below cover all three sides — the planning practice that writes the rules, the entitlement work that navigates them, the market data that tracks the pipeline, and the reform evidence that shows which changes actually produce homes.
